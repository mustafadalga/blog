Lord Acton's famous quote, "Power tends to corrupt, and absolute power corrupts absolutely," has been proven right over and over again in history. Whenever people who used to be oppressed finally get power in their hands, they usually start doing the exact same cruel things to others.

One clear example is Israel. Jewish people went through terrible suffering and genocide during World War II. But after the state of Israel was created and they gained military power, the violence and oppression they put on Palestinians is now called a genocide by a lot of people. Yesterday's victims turned into today's oppressors once they got the power.

The exact same thing happened during the Chinese Cultural Revolution. The movement under Mao Zedong started out promising equality and justice. But once they got control, they crushed anyone who disagreed with them. They became just as brutal as the rulers they fought against and caused massive harm to their own people.

The Soviet Union was no different. Before the revolution, the working class was oppressed and treated badly. Once they took power under Stalin, they built a brutal dictatorship where millions of people were thrown into Gulag camps, exiled, or killed. The people who were once victims did the exact same thing when they sat in the seat of power.

By nature, humans lose control when they have unlimited power. Anywhere there are no strict checks, balances, or limits on authority, relying on a leader's good intentions or mercy always fails. No matter who gets absolute power, it always ends the exact same way.
