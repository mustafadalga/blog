Most people assume that going to good schools, getting degrees, or reading a lot automatically makes someone a good person. That is completely wrong. When it comes to character, education does not mean anything. A bad person is a bad person everywhere, under any condition.

You can see this clearly if you look at real life and history. High-level politicians, diplomats who committed massive crimes against humanity, or Machiavellian figures like Bill Gates, Elon Musk, and Vladimir Putin are all highly educated people. Even in movies and TV shows, the worst villains like Malcolm Merlyn or Wilson Fisk aren't stupid, they are smart and well educated.

Education never stopped any of these people from doing terrible things. In fact it does the exact opposite. It gives them more opportunities, helps them brainstorm better strategies, and widens their knowledge so they can do much bigger damage.

An uneducated bad person can only cause limited trouble around their own neighborhood. But a highly educated, Machiavellian bad person knows how to use the law, the economy, media, or technology to exploit millions of people, and usually gets away with it without going to jail.

Education does not build morals, it only gives you tools and knowledge. In the hands of a bad person, that knowledge just makes them much more dangerous and effective at doing bad things.
