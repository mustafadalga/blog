The world keeps changing, so every generation grows up under completely different conditions than the one before. One thing never changes though, older generations always complain about younger people.

I ran into this exact thing at a restaurant recently. The head cook there started telling me that young people today are lazy, useless, and just complain all the time without wanting to work. Meanwhile in his day, he said they worked non-stop without whining. Everyone hears this exact speech all the time, but it ignores how much things actually changed.

Back in the day, if you learned one trade or set of skills, you could use that same knowledge for thirty years and build a whole career. Today technology moves so fast and competition is so high that if you stop learning for a year, you fall behind. You constantly have to update your skills just to keep a job, which creates constant stress and anxiety about the future.

The economic reality is completely different too. Older generations had way better purchasing power. A normal job could actually pay for a house or support a family. Young people today face insane living costs, zero job security, and a future where basic stability feels way out of reach.

So when older people judge young workers using the rules of thirty years ago and call them lazy, it doesn't make any sense. You can't compare two completely different worlds like they are the same.
