Some people argue that letting regular citizens carry guns makes everyone safer, but in reality it does the opposite. Making it easy for everyone to own guns doesn't bring security, it just makes daily life more dangerous.

First of all, this mostly helps criminals and bad people. Criminals already know how to get illegal weapons and usually have way more experience using them than an ordinary citizen. When guns are legal and easy to buy, you just open up legal ways for them to get weapons too. In a real street situation, a criminal is still going to be one step ahead of a normal person.

Then there is the training issue. Handling a gun safely and staying calm under pressure takes real training and regular practice. Most ordinary people don't have the time or energy for that. A gun in the hands of someone without proper training causes accidents and stupid deaths rather than protection.

Also nobody actually feels safe living in a place where anyone around them could be carrying a weapon. Instead of safety, it creates constant paranoia and fear. People start looking at each other with suspicion, and a simple argument in traffic can turn deadly in two seconds.

It makes police work much harder and more dangerous too. Police officers have to assume every single person they stop might be armed, which increases the risk of things going wrong for both the police and regular citizens.

So individual gun ownership doesn't protect people, it just turns everyday situations into tense and deadly risks.
