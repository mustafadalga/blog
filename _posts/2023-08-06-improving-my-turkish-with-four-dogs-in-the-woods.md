When I moved to Istanbul to study at Marmara University, I realized my Turkish wasn't smooth enough. I used to freeze up during class presentations and couldn't talk fluently when hanging out with friends.

To fix this I decided to copy my friend Uğurcan, who spoke Turkish really well and smoothly. He read a lot of books, so I started reading books too. But I didn't read quietly in my head, I started reading them out loud.

The best part was doing this in the woods near my student dorm with four dogs. Every day I walked into the forest with those four dogs, found a quiet spot, and read my book out loud while they just hung out next to me.

I kept doing this for four years. By the end of it my Turkish got way better. Now I can speak without getting stuck like before and write blog posts comfortably.

Everything started just by sitting under a tree in the woods with four dogs and reading out loud every day. Now I can express myself much better.
