We are not robots, we are emotional beings. To get through hard times and keep going, everyone needs something to hold onto. What that thing is looks different for every person, but the need itself is the same.

For a lot of people it is religion. Islam, Christianity, or any other faith gives people a clear reason to live, a community, and comfort when things go badly.

Other people don't follow religion and instead attach themselves to political or social ideologies. Ideas like socialism, communism, liberalism, feminism, or anarchism become their main focus. They look at the world through those ideas and spend their energy fighting for them.

Some people find that connection in admiring someone else, like a musician or an athlete, or belonging to a specific club or online community. Others find it in nature and animals. Taking care of a pet, working in a garden, or trying to protect the environment gives them a reason to care every day.

It is the same for atheists too. Not believing in a god doesn't mean having no purpose. Many atheists put that exact same energy into science, helping people, or caring for animals. The specific label doesn't really matter, what matters is having something you care about.

No matter how tough or cold someone looks from the outside, if they have nothing left to believe in or care about, they eventually lose their strength and will to keep fighting. Because we aren't machines, we need some kind of belief or passion just to keep going.
