Setting up a ballot box and letting people vote does not make a country a democracy on its own. For a democracy to actually work, it depends on how people get their information, whether they are financially independent, and who truly controls public money. There are concrete reasons why democracy never functions in certain countries.

The first major barrier is media control and state propaganda. In an authoritarian system, the ruling party controls the television networks, newspapers, and internet access. They silence opposition voices and push their own narratives all day long. Ordinary citizens work long hours just to feed their families and survive. Nobody has the time, energy, or tools to sit down and fact-check every piece of news they hear. When people hear the exact same story repeated everywhere every single day, they believe it. You cannot have a fair vote when information channels are blocked.

Then there is a huge psychological trick around public money: politicians using the citizens' own money to act like generous benefactors. People pay nearly half of what they earn in taxes. That money does not belong to the president or the mayor; it comes straight out of the pockets of ordinary workers. Yet, when politicians use a tiny fraction of those taxes to build a road, a school, or a hospital, they present it like a personal favor or an act of charity.

The absurd part is that citizens actually feel grateful to politicians for services paid for with their own money. A politician's only job is to spend public tax revenue on public needs. There is nothing to thank them for, and citizens owe them zero gratitude. Some people claim that big corporations pay the most taxes, but that argument is completely flawed. Large corporations use roads, water, power grids, and natural resources thousands of times more than an ordinary family does, while polluting the environment in the process. Compared to the massive resources they consume, big companies should be paying far higher taxes than working people.

Economic poverty makes this dependency worse. Rulers first make people poor through heavy taxes, inflation, and bad economic policies. Then, they hand back a tiny crumb of that collected wealth as free food boxes or winter coal. People who are struggling to survive become dependent on these basic handouts and vote for the people giving them out. A society forced to beg for crumbs bought with its own money cannot make a free choice at the ballot box.

When you combine that with a lack of independent courts and blind loyalty to leaders or tribes, the system breaks down completely. Without neutral judges, honest police, and a free press, elections simply decide which group gets to loot the national budget for the next four or five years.

Putting a ballot box in a country where the media is controlled, where people do not understand that the budget belongs to them, and where politicians act like masters giving out charity will never produce a democracy. It will only produce elected autocrats.
