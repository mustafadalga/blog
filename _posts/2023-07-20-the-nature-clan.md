The Nature Clan represents everything and every place that belongs to nature, where nature exists.

Ever since I started attending university, contrary to what is generally assumed, I’ve been spending most of my time not around campus, but within the Nature Clan.

As a person dedicated to learning, my friendships formed in the Nature Clan, the strong bonds and the lessons learned from my prolonged observations of animal behaviors have led to an increasing appreciation for the Nature Clan each passing day.

![]({{ '/assets/images/092fe84a-d340-485f-a353-362065351bb5.jpeg' | relative_url }})

As far as I can remember, I’ve always been in a monk-like pursuit. The lives within the Nature Clan, animal behaviors, their communications with each other, their struggle for survival, have all been incredibly instructive for better understanding and improving myself. The persistence and fighting spirit I acquired from the Nature Clan greatly influence my attitude when developing software; it’s what keeps me persistently grappling with issues that can take hours or days to resolve, without giving up.

![]({{ '/assets/images/dbae7db4-6614-4046-9cf8-326a1f1f44a9.jpeg' | relative_url }})

This piece serves as an introduction to the [Nature Clan](https://www.instagram.com/dogaklani/). I hope to begin telling the life stories within the Nature Clan soon.
