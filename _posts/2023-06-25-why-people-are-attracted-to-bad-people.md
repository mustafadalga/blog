It isn't actually hard to understand why some people keep getting attracted to selfish, manipulative, or dangerous people. Even when they know that person uses others for their own benefit, the attraction still stays there.

One big reason is boredom and wanting excitement. Good, stable people often feel boring and predictable. Someone who lives recklessly and breaks rules looks much more exciting and adventurous, especially when you are young. On top of that there is the forbidden fruit effect, when society says someone is bad news or off limits, people want them even more.

Low self-esteem and past trauma play a huge role too. People who don't value themselves often feel like they deserve to be treated badly, so toxic behavior feels normal to them. Others walk in thinking they can fix or save the bad person. That almost always fails because nobody changes unless they actually want to change themselves.

There is also the illusion of strength. People who act arrogant, selfish, and cruel are often seen as dominant and powerful. Some people get attracted to that because being around someone like that makes them feel like they have higher status too.

So getting pulled toward toxic people isn't some big mystery. Whether it is chasing excitement, having low self-worth, wanting to fix someone, or confusing cruelty with strength, people keep ending up in the same bad relationships for pretty clear reasons.
