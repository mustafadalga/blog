What people imagine in their heads and how the real world actually works are often completely different things. This is especially true for leaders and people in high positions. They end up living inside a bubble where they only see what they want to see.

For example, when a president or a company CEO decides to visit somewhere, everything gets cleaned up and staged beforehand. Subordinates and lower managers act like lickspittles to flatter the boss and protect their own jobs, hiding every single broken thing. The person visiting looks at that one day of fake perfection and thinks everything always runs smoothly, wondering why regular people even complain. The minute they leave, everything goes right back to normal.

It isn't just about visits though. People at the top usually prefer seeing the world through their own beliefs rather than looking at ugly facts. And nobody around them is going to ruin the mood by telling the truth, because everyone just wants to keep their position and please the boss.

When leaders don't see what is actually happening on the ground, every decision they make is based on filtered and fake information. They stay safe inside their bubble, while ordinary people are the ones who deal with the bad results, broken systems, and economic mess those decisions create.
