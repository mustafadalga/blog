Things you see and touch every day feel ordinary. But things you cannot see, things that stay far away and closed off, grow bigger inside people's heads.

You can see this clearly with political leaders. Abdullah Öcalan has been isolated for years, his voice is barely ever heard, and physically he is completely out of sight. This creates a kind of mystery and mystical power around him for his followers. Because he is never around, it makes it easy for people to build legends about him.

It works the same way with leaders like Vladimir Putin and Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. One keeps his image through tough-guy shows and acting like a closed box, the other stays behind palaces, huge security armies, and a strict distance. None of these leaders mix with normal citizens on the street. They keep that distance on purpose because if someone walks past you every day, you immediately realize they are just a regular person. As long as they stay out of reach, they look powerful.

The exact same rule applies to celebrities. People like Rihanna, Kim Kardashian, or Selena Gomez look perfect and extraordinary on screens. If you saw them every morning buying bread at your neighborhood bakery, that whole magic would completely disappear in a few days.

Religious beliefs work in a similar way too. People attach themselves much more easily to things they cannot see with their own eyes and have no physical proof of. Everyone can project whatever meaning they want onto an invisible power inside their own head.

The mechanism is really that simple: the further away and more closed off a figure stays, the more power people imagine they have. The moment you get close and see how ordinary they are, the illusion breaks.
