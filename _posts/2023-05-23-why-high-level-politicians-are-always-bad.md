People always wonder why politicians in high positions are almost always bad and corrupt. The answer is pretty simple, the system itself requires you to be that way just to reach the top. You have to be Machiavellian. You have to be willing to lie, make dirty deals, and do literally anything to get power.

An ethical and honest person cannot climb that ladder. If you only tell the truth and refuse to play dirty games, you get filtered out at the very beginning. To reach a high position in politics, you basically have to be a bad person and some kind of bastard. Of course there are a few rare exceptions of good people making it, but rare examples don't change the reality of how the game works.

Even if a truly good person somehow reached the top by accident, look at reality. Do you really think the oligarchs, rich elites, and other corrupt people will let you stay in that seat if you don't serve them? No chance. They pay for the campaigns, they control the media, and they expect favors back. If you refuse to protect their corrupt interests, they will get rid of you quickly.

On top of that, regular voters usually don't help either. Most people prefer listening to fake promises rather than hard truths, so a manipulative politician always gets more support than an honest one.

So high-level politicians being terrible isn't an accident. Being ruthless and serving corrupt interests is literally a requirement to get the job and keep it.
