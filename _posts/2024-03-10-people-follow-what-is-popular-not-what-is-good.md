There is a good example in Mark Twain's story "The Million Pound Bank Note." In the story, a totally ordinary restaurant suddenly turns into a famous hotspot just because a millionaire named Henry Adams ate there. The food or quality didn't change at all, crowds just started showing up because someone rich went there.

On social media today, the exact same thing happens every day. Whenever a celebrity or an influencer promotes a product or visits a cafe, huge crowds blindly copy them without thinking twice.

Popular tourist spots like Ortaköy in Istanbul are an easy example of this. People pack into these places mainly because they are trending online and everyone takes photos there. Most of the time it has nothing to do with the actual quality of the place or the food, people just go there for the visual appeal and to get likes.

When everybody blindly chases whatever is trending, people stop figuring out what they actually like on their own. Instead of judging quality by their own experience, they just follow the crowd, and everyone ends up liking and doing the exact same things.
