Recently, a YouTuber Diamond Tema in Turkey got serious death threats and an arrest warrant just for speaking critically about Sharia law and the Prophet Muhammad. This is just one example of what happens in Turkey when someone openly questions religious beliefs or popular ideas.

When people aren't allowed to ask questions or criticize dogmas, society gets stuck. Take a country like Estonia for example. People there openly question things, hold leaders accountable, and criticize systems without getting arrested or threatened. Because nobody is scared to ask questions, there is transparency and things actually improve.

In Turkey it works the exact opposite way. Certain religious groups and politicians push blind faith and treat any criticism as a crime. They don't want people asking questions or thinking critically. When a society is trained to believe blindly without questioning anything, bad people easily take advantage of that and manipulate everyone.

Silencing people with arrest warrants and threats doesn't protect society, it just keeps everyone afraid to think for themselves and lets the same people stay in control.
