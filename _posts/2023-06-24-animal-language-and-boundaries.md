Communication is not something only humans do. Humans use words and body language, and animals also have their own ways of talking and their own boundaries. If you want to get along with an animal, you first need to understand what their signals mean.

Think about how it works with people in daily life. If you are sitting at a cafe eating something and a random stranger walks up, sits right at your table without asking, or walks into your house like they live there, you are going to get angry and start a fight. Normally people respect each other's personal space.

Animals have the exact same kind of social rules and boundaries. If you walk up to a dog you don't know and grab its tail, and the dog doesn't want that, it might snap or bite you. The dog isn't just being crazy, that is literally how it communicates. It is telling you that you crossed its boundary and need to back off.

Every animal species has its own signals. Wolves howl to talk to each other and mark their territory. Cats use their tails, puff up their fur, or hiss when they feel threatened or annoyed.

Animals show what they feel and what they need through these signals. Once you pay attention to their body language and respect their limits, communicating with them is pretty straightforward and you avoid unnecessary problems.
