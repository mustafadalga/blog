People have certain sensitivities and alarm buttons. If you keep playing with those sensitivities for no real reason, after a while you break them. Eventually people get numb and start treating real emergencies like they are totally normal.

You see a very simple example of this in traffic. Ambulances, police cars, and fire trucks often turn on their sirens even when there is no real emergency, just to get through traffic faster. Drivers notice this and over time they stop taking sirens seriously. Then when there is a real life-or-death emergency, people don't react fast anymore.

The exact same thing happens at work. Because we work remotely, our team uses Slack for communication. Some coworkers constantly send me fragmented, unnecessary messages one after another for tiny coding issues that aren't urgent at all. Because my Slack is always blowing up, when there is an actual urgent problem it is hard for me to even notice or take it seriously.

Politics and religion work the exact same way. Political parties constantly abuse people's national feelings for cheap votes. In Turkey, because most people are Muslim, religious groups and politicians keep using religion to manipulate everyone. After getting played like that over and over, people lose those sensitivities and stop trusting anyone.

It is the same in the economy and education too. When citizens get used to non-stop inflation every single month, they become numb to economic crises. As the Turkish Lira keeps losing value, people lose trust in their own currency and move their money elsewhere. Constant instability in the education system also kills students' and parents' trust in schools.

Whether people do it on purpose or by accident, once you overuse someone's emotional triggers and alarm buttons, all that is left is numbness and zero trust.
